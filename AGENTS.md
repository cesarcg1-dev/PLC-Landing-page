
## Page architecture
- Keep the one-page experience at the index route with anchor navigation, because the brief explicitly requests a single scrolling page.
- Keep section components in the industrial module and editable business data in its content module, so mock content can be replaced without altering the page structure.
- Keep the contact submission browser-only and explicitly simulated; no data is stored or sent until a real service is requested.
- Serve downloaded photographs through asset pointers to keep binary media out of the repository.
